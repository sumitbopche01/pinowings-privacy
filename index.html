Privacy Policy — PinoWings

Last updated: October 27, 2026
Package: com.HNStudio.PinoWings
Developer: HN Studio

Summary

PinoWings does not require an account. The game works offline. We do not sell your personal information.

Data on your device

Progress is saved locally: scores, coins, ships, upgrades, ranks, missiles, drones, paint, level progress and stars, Xenotech, codex entries, the daily challenge and missions, achievements, play statistics and settings. Uninstalling or clearing app data removes it.

Data we do not collect

  • No account or email
  • No location tracking
  • No ads or ad IDs
  • No real-money purchases
  • No analytics in the default release build

Permissions

Optional vibration for gameplay feedback. Internet is not required to play.

Contact

Email: sumit@harinext.com

Changes

If we add online analytics or accounts, this page will be updated before those features ship.
